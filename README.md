#  Project AURA — Intelligent Scents for a Harmonized Self
### L'Oréal Brandstorm 2026 | Luxury Fragrance Innovation

> *"Your Scent DNA. Your Wellness Ritual. Your L'Oréal Luxe."*


## Overview
📎 [View Full Pitch Deck](./L'Oreal%20Brandstorm%20submission.pdf)

Project AURA is a strategic innovation proposal developed for the **L'Oréal Brandstorm 2026** competition. It reimagines the luxury fragrance experience by introducing the **Scent Intelligence Layer (SIL)** — an AI-powered ecosystem that personalizes fragrance not just for allure, but for **emotional well-being**.

The concept integrates **Neuro-Mapping**, **Aromachology**, and a **circular refillable hardware system** to bridge the gap between luxury beauty and mental wellness.

##  The Solution — The AURA Ecosystem

### 1. Scent Intelligence Layer (SIL)
An AI engine that builds a living **Scent DNA profile** for each user through two paths:

- **The Wellness Path** — Uses Neuro-Mapping to recommend functional scents combating anxiety, brain fog, or restlessness
  - *Sandalwood/Vetiver* → Grounding during anxiety
  - *Citrus/Peppermint* → Cognitive focus & clarity
  - *Lavender/Vanilla* → Cortisol reduction & calm

- **The Identity Path** — Bypasses industry jargon (Chypre, Sillage) and uses lifestyle inputs (music, fashion, travel) to curate a daily signature scent

### 2. The AURA Scent-Sync Bracelet (Hardware)
A high-fashion, refillable wearable that houses your Scent DNA:
- Discreet touch-release mechanism for wrist and neck
- **The Carry Core** — a magnetic 5–8ml mini bottle that lives inside the main bottle; travel-safe and spill-proof
- Precision refills via personalized **Aura Pods**

### 3.  ScentCircle — Community Platform
The first platform built around **fragrance identity**:
- Users create Scent DNA profiles and connect with others who share similar olfactive taste
- Share layering combinations as community "recipes"
- **Scent School** — AI-powered fragrance education, evolving users from casual wearers to informed enthusiasts
